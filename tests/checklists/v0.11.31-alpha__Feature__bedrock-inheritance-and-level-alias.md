# Human Test Checklist: dazzlecmd inheriting dazzle-lib 0.8.1 (+ the level value-alias)

**Target version:** dazzlecmd `0.11.31-alpha` (`dccae3c`) on dazzle-lib `0.8.1` (`a49f6c1`) + dazzlecmd-lib `0.10.15-alpha` (`4cf2d44`)
**Companion checklist:** the bedrock-level edges live in dazzle-lib's `v0.8.1__Feature__nucleus-rank-addressing-and-level-alias.md` — THIS document covers what the APP inherits: every live `dz` surface that rides Groupable/Continuum machinery after the first bedrock change of the arc.
**Companion automated tests:** app 1570 + byte-gate 10/10 (already green — this checklist is the live-surface complement).

## What this is

dazzle-lib 0.8.1 added a FIELD to the frozen `Continuum` dataclass (`subtype`), changed `densify_between`'s signature, and added five methods. dazzlecmd consumes these types through `VERB_SPACE` (all kit lifecycle verbs), `LEVEL_CONTINUUM` (dz level + target resolution), `KIT_PRESENCE_SPACE` (visibility/activation/mode), the interrogation cards, and the derived FQCN tree (`fqcn_tree` walks these objects and reads `levels()`/`rank()`). A field addition to a frozen dataclass can surprise anything that iterates fields, serializes, reprs, or constructs positionally. This checklist walks every inheriting surface live. All writes against an isolated config.

## Prerequisites

Isolate first — cmd.exe: `set DAZZLECMD_CONFIG=%TEMP%\ck1131\config.json` · PowerShell: `$env:DAZZLECMD_CONFIG = "$env:TEMP\ck1131\config.json"` · POSIX: `export DAZZLECMD_CONFIG=/tmp/ck1131/config.json`. Reset: delete that dir. Never touch `~/.dz`.
